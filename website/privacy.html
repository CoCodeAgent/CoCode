一、我们如何收集和使用个人信息

1. How We Collect and Use Personal Information

1.1 账号注册与登录

1.1 Account Registration and Login

当你注册或登录 CoCode 时，我们会收集你的电子邮箱地址，用于创建账号、身份验证及服务通知。如你使用第三方账号登录，经你同意后，我们会获取该第三方平台的公开信息（如昵称、头像）用于账号绑定。上述信息均为提供账号服务所必需，如你拒绝提供，将无法注册并使用我们的账号体系。

When you register or log in to CoCode, we collect your email address for account creation, identity verification, and service notifications. If you log in using a third-party account, with your consent, we obtain public information from that platform (e.g., nickname, avatar) for account binding. The above information is necessary for providing account services; if you refuse to provide it, you will not be able to register and use our account system.

1.2 服务运行与安全

1.2 Service Operation and Security

为保障服务正常运行和网络安全，我们会收集你的设备信息（操作系统类型及版本、设备型号）、IP 地址、软件版本号、网络接入方式及操作日志。这些信息是提供服务和保障安全所必需的基本信息。我们会将上述信息用于安全事件的监测与排查、异常登录的识别以及服务稳定性的统计分析，并将按照最低必要原则进行去标识化处理。

To ensure normal service operation and network security, we collect your device information (OS type and version, device model), IP address, software version, network access type, and operation logs. This information is essential for providing the Service and maintaining security. We use such information for security incident monitoring and investigation, identification of abnormal logins, and statistical analysis of service stability, and will process it on a minimum-necessary and de-identified basis.

1.3 模型调用与对话数据

1.3 Model Calls and Conversation Data

当你使用 AI 功能时，你输入的文本、代码片段、任务指令及上传的文件会从你的设备直接发送至你自行配置的模型提供商，以生成响应。

When you use AI features, the text, code snippets, task instructions, and files you upload are sent directly from your device to the model provider you configure, in order to generate responses.

本地优先原则：你的代码库和对话内容默认存储在你的本地设备上。我们不会主动上传你的代码库文件。仅当你使用云同步、跨端同步等功能时，相关数据才会传输至我们的服务器。

Local-first principle: Your codebase and conversations are stored on your local device by default. We do not proactively upload your codebase files. Data is transmitted to our servers only when you use features such as cloud sync or cross-device sync.

1.4 免费语音识别

1.4 Free Speech Recognition

使用免费语音识别时，录音会经由我们的服务器转发至语音识别服务商以生成文字。该功能免费；我们不会将录音用于模型训练或其他目的。

When you use free speech recognition, the recording is relayed through our server to the speech-recognition provider to generate text. This feature is free; we do not use recordings for model training or other purposes.

1.5 客服与反馈

1.5 Customer Support and Feedback

当你联系客服或提交反馈时，我们会收集你的联系方式及你主动提供的问题描述，用于响应你的请求和改进服务。为便于定位和解决问题，你可能会在反馈中附上日志文件或截图，请你自行确认其中不包含不愿提供给他人的信息后再行提交。

When you contact customer support or submit feedback, we collect your contact information and the problem description you provide, in order to respond to your request and improve the Service. To help locate and resolve issues, you may attach log files or screenshots to your feedback; please make sure they do not contain information you do not wish to share before submitting.

Under applicable laws and regulations, we do not need your authorization or consent to collect and use your personal information in the following circumstances: (1) where it is necessary for the conclusion or performance of a contract to which you are a party; (2) where it is necessary for the performance of statutory duties or obligations; (3) where it is necessary for responding to a public health emergency, or for protecting the life, health, or property of a natural person in an emergency; (4) where personal information is processed within a reasonable scope for news reporting or public opinion supervision in the public interest; (5) where personal information that you have voluntarily disclosed or that has been lawfully disclosed is processed within a reasonable scope as provided by law; and (6) other circumstances provided by laws and regulations. 5. How We Protect Personal Information Security

5.1 安全措施

5.1 Security Measures

我们采取行业通行的安全措施保护你的个人信息，包括：传输加密（TLS）、存储加密、最小权限的访问控制、安全审计及数据分类分级管理。API Key 等敏感凭证在本地通过操作系统安全存储（如 macOS Keychain）加密保存，不以明文形式暴露。我们的员工均签署保密协议，并仅能在履行职责所必需的范围内访问个人信息。

Sensitive credentials such as API keys are encrypted locally using the operating system's secure storage (e.g., macOS Keychain) and are not exposed in plaintext. Our employees have signed confidentiality agreements and can only access personal information within the scope necessary for performing their duties.

5.2 Security Incident Response

Although we adopt reasonable security measures, the Internet environment is not 100% secure. Please keep your account credentials and local devices safe; losses caused by your own fault (e.g., voluntarily disclosing your password, or a stolen device without screen lock enabled) shall be borne by you. In the unfortunate event of a personal information security incident, we will immediately activate our emergency response plan, report to the relevant competent authorities as required by law, and promptly inform you of the incident, its possible impact, and remedial measures you can take, via in-app notifications, email, and other means.
