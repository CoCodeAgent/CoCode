一、术语定义

1. Definitions

为便于理解，本协议中的下列术语含义如下：

For ease of understanding, the following terms in this Agreement have the meanings set out below:

  • "本服务"：指 CoCode 桌面应用、命令行工具（CLI）、官方网站及相关配套服务；
  • "Service": refers to the CoCode desktop application, command-line interface (CLI), official website, and related supporting services;
  • "个人信息"：指以电子或者其他方式记录的与已识别或者可识别的自然人有关的各种信息，不包括匿名化处理后的信息；
  • "Personal information": refers to various kinds of information, recorded electronically or otherwise, relating to identified or identifiable natural persons, excluding information after anonymization;
  • "敏感个人信息"：指一旦泄露或者非法使用，容易导致自然人的人格尊严受到侵害或者人身、财产安全受到危害的个人信息，包括生物识别、宗教信仰、特定身份、医疗健康、金融账户、行踪轨迹等信息，以及不满十四周岁未成年人的个人信息；
  • "Sensitive personal information": refers to personal information that, once leaked or illegally used, could easily cause harm to a natural person's dignity or to the safety of his or her person or property, including biometric information, religious beliefs, specific identity, medical health, financial accounts, location tracks, and any personal information of minors under the age of fourteen;
  • "个人信息主体"：指个人信息所识别或者关联的自然人，即本服务的用户（"你"）；
  • "Personal information subject": refers to the natural person identified by or associated with the personal information, i.e., the user of the Service ("you");
  • "单独同意"：指个人信息处理者在作出处理行为的告知后，专门针对个人信息处理活动取得个人作出同意的意思表示，区别于概括同意或一揽子同意；
  • "Separate consent": refers to an expression of consent specifically given by the individual for a particular processing activity after being informed of such processing, as distinguished from general or bundled consent;
  • "匿名化"：指个人信息经过处理无法识别特定自然人且不能复原的过程；
  • "Anonymization": refers to a process by which personal information cannot be used to identify a specific natural person and cannot be restored;
  • "境内"：指中华人民共和国关境以内（不包含香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区）；
  • "Onshore": refers to within the customs territory of the People's Republic of China (excluding the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, and the Taiwan region);
  • "境外"：指中华人民共和国关境以外的国家和地区。
  • "Overseas": refers to countries and regions outside the customs territory of the People's Republic of China.

三、个人信息跨境传输与处理的目的

3. Purposes of Cross-Border Transfer and Processing

我们进行个人信息跨境传输及处理的目的包括：

The purposes of cross-border transfer and processing of your personal information include:

  1. 网站与接口服务交付：通过部署于境外网络的 CDN 与基础设施，向你提供官方网站的访问、静态资源分发及服务接口响应；

  12. Security audit and compliance: Meeting the needs of laws and regulations, regulatory requirements, and security audits.

我们不会将你的个人信息用于上述目的之外的其他目的。如需变更处理目的，我们将重新取得你的单独同意。

We will not use your personal information for purposes other than those listed above. If we need to change the processing purpose, we will obtain your separate consent again.

四、跨境传输与处理的个人信息类型

4. Types of Personal Information Transferred and Processed Cross-Border

我们跨境传输和处理的个人信息与处理目的直接相关，遵循最小必要原则，具体包括：

The personal information we transfer and process cross-border is directly related to the processing purposes and follows the principle of minimization, specifically including:

上述信息为跨境传输信息的全部类别。我们不会传输你的完整代码库、本地文件目录结构或与处理目的无关的其他信息，具体传输的类别以你实际使用服务的情况为准。

The above constitutes the full categories of information transferred cross-border. We will not transfer your entire codebase, local file directory structure, or other information unrelated to the processing purposes; the specific categories transferred depend on your actual use of the Service.

In accordance with Article 38(1)(i) of the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, we carry out this cross-border transfer of personal information on the basis of your separate consent, and we will continuously assess the applicability of the following compliance pathways: the security assessment organized by the national cyberspace administration; the personal information protection certification conducted by professional institutions; or the conclusion of a contract with the overseas recipient according to the standard contract formulated by the national cyberspace administration.

If relevant laws and regulations impose new requirements on outbound transfer of personal information, we will promptly adjust this Agreement and the data transfer arrangements to ensure continued compliance.

七、个人信息存储与安全保障

7. Personal Information Storage and Security

7.1 存储地点与期限

7.1 Storage Location and Retention

你的账号与积分数据以中华人民共和国境内服务器为主要存储地。经由境外网络（如 CDN、网关节点）处理的传输中数据，仅在实现处理目的所必需的期限内保留，境外接收方不会建立你个人信息的长期存储副本，除非其服务机制要求的最短缓存周期。当个人信息超出保存期限或达到你注销账户的条件时，我们将对个人信息进行删除或匿名化处理，法律另有规定的除外。

Your account and credit data are primarily stored on servers located within the People's Republic of China. Data in transit processed through overseas networks (such as CDN or gateway nodes) is retained only for the period necessary to achieve the processing purpose. The overseas recipients will not establish long-term storage copies of your personal information, except for the shortest caching period required by their service mechanisms. When personal information exceeds the retention period or when your account is deleted, we will delete or anonymize the personal information, except as otherwise provided by law.

7.2 安全保障措施

7.2 Security Safeguards

我们采取以下技术与管理措施保障你个人信息在跨境传输中的安全性：

We adopt the following technical and organizational measures to ensure the security of your personal information during cross-border transfer:

  • 传输加密：所有数据传输均采用 TLS 加密通道，防止传输过程中被窃取或篡改；
  • Encryption in transit: All data transfers use TLS-encrypted channels to prevent interception or tampering during transmission;
  • 密码保护：你的登录密码使用单向加盐哈希算法存储，任何人员（包括我们内部员工）均无法查看你的明文密码；
  • Password protection: Your login password is stored using a one-way salted hashing algorithm; no one (including our internal staff) can view your plaintext password;
  • 密钥隔离：你的自定义模型 API 密钥仅存储在你本地设备，我们的服务器不保存、不备份你的密钥明文；
  • Key isolation: Your custom model API keys are stored only on your local device; our servers do not store or back up the plaintext of your keys;
  • 最小必要原则：仅传输实现服务目的所必需的信息，不传输与目的无关的个人信息；
  • Minimization principle: Only information necessary for the service purpose is transferred; personal information unrelated to the purpose is not transferred;
  • 脱敏处理：调用记录等日志信息中不包含你输入的明文内容；
  • De-identification: Log information such as call records does not contain the plaintext of your input;
  • 人机验证：在登录、注册等关键环节部署人机验证机制，防范自动化攻击；
  • Human verification: Human verification mechanisms are deployed at critical steps such as login and registration to defend against automated attacks;
  • 供应商合规：我们要求境外接收方采取符合行业标准的安全措施（如加密、访问控制、安全审计），并仅在授权范围内处理信息，不得转委托处理；
  • Vendor compliance: We require overseas recipients to adopt industry-standard security measures (such as encryption, access control, and security audits), process information only within the authorized scope, and refrain from further entrusting the processing;
  • 访问控制：我们内部对可访问相关数据的范围进行严格限制，并定期开展安全审查与应急演练。
  • Access control: We strictly limit the internal scope of access to relevant data and conduct regular security reviews and emergency drills.
